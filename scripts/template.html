      How the scoring works
      • Cumulative Rank (headline): each week, publishers on the list are ordered by that week's List Rank points. With N publishers on the list, the best scores N and the worst scores 1. Publishers tied in a week share the higher score. Weekly scores add up over the year.
      • List Rank points: #1 earns 15 points, #2 earns 14, down to 1 point for #15, summed over every appearance.
      • Appearances: one point for every weekly slot a publisher fills.
      • Titles: distinct books a publisher has placed on the list.
      • Average rank: mean chart position across every appearance. Lower is better.
      • Publishers are named exactly as the NYT lists them, so imprints such as Little, Brown and Mulholland are counted separately. "T" marks a tie.
      • A list counts toward __SEASON__ if its NYT list date falls in __SEASON__.
